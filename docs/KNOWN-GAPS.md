## Blocked on one ruling or one number (each cites its CLAUDE.md entry)

- **mez_in** - one number ('how often does content mez you') unblocks ~289 powers scoring nothing today: self mez protection (landed, inert), ally mez protection and resistance (Clear Mind, Clarity, Thaw...), and the debuff-resistance families. The single highest-leverage open ruling.
- **Spawn rank mix** - minion/lieutenant/boss shares at typical settings. Unlocks the crit premium above the 5% floor and would sharpen rank-dependent accuracy terms.
- **Operating health** - the 13 health-scaling powers (SR scaling resistance, Gamma Boost) are decoded and pinned; a linear curve's honest average is its midpoint, so 50% is the derived recommendation.
- **Stack meters** - BuildStatic, BuildFrenzy, Contaminated, Disintegrate, the combo systems, Bio's stance: one scenario constant each, or one ruling for the class. Titan Weapons' Momentum is this class too: Whirling Smash and Follow Through are usable ONLY inside the Momentum window (client short help 'Requires Momentum'; v46 zeroes them for AFK), so active TW farming pays a structural tax - the set cannot chain high-cap AoEs freely (Maelwys, topic 64761). Pricing the fast-form uptime + the gate for ACTIVE play is the remaining piece.
- **The +5x8 AFK tier** - Maelwys: +5x8 (50+1 vs 55 in AE) is the real ceiling players farm at and both Fiery Aura builds can pass it with fully passive builds. Our sustain ladder stops at +4x8; extending it needs the +5 enemy accuracy/DPS numbers for the scenario table.
- **Stone Armor farm candidate** - passively caps res+def with Rooted regen, Brimstone damage procs and Minerals recharge (Maelwys's RadM/Stone example: 49 HP/s passive, Atom Smasher auto-fire). Blocked on the aura/patch proc valuation ruling; certify a Stone Armor alternative when that lands.
- **Regen model: VERIFIED CORRECT end to end** (2026-08-17, retraction of the same-day 'under-captured' alarm - both supporting probes were bad: a phantom piece uid slotting nothing, then a wrong-keyed census). tools/census_power_regen.py is the standing instrument: 146 client self-regen records in served sets, 125 carried, 21 gaps all known classes (Gamma Boost health-scaling ruling x5; alias/liveness records like Rime and Reactive Regeneration to cross-check against the missing-powers pins - never blind-add). Maelwys's RadM/Stone example measures +4x8 FULLY PASSIVE (37.7 HP/s) at contribution 199.7 in OUR model via the faithful converter (coh-scorer-lab/measure_maelwys.py) - the tier-vs-objective ruling's live exhibit, no model work prerequisite after all.
- **AFK offense is unmodelled** (Maelwys 2026-08-16, confirmed in our artifacts): the farm_afk SUSTAIN ledger correctly allows regen + ONE non-interruptible auto-fire power, but the OFFENSE channel still prices the full click attack chain an AFK farmer cannot cast - the TW/Bio champion certified on Fire Ball/Char clicks and skipped Bio's damage aura. An AFK damage rule (damage auras + the one auto-fire + procs only) is a model change and a farm re-cert; Joel's ruling.
- **Domination's magnitude half** - adds or replaces is ambiguous in the client (3 of 41 pairs fit neither reading). Duration half is shipped; magnitude deliberately not guessed.
- **Fiery Embrace's type question** - the client grants a FIRE-typed buff; our damage_buff is type-blind. Measure before touching; adding the Fire templates first is the known inflation trap.
- **The ally channel** - ally mez, ally absorb, ally slow resistance share one unbuilt channel; build it once when mez_in lands.
- **Fighting-pool cross-boost** - Boxing/Kick/Cross Punch each stronger for owning the others; real, named, unmodelled.
- **Power Boost's Set_Mode** - a 15-second amplifier mode, the same capability class as Fury; roster known via tags, uptime is the gap.
- **~460 target-cap/radius rows** - client disagreements never classified; blanket-sync is known-wrong (pseudo-pet folds), so each needs adjudication.
