## The second accuracy pass

Six model versions of engine accuracy work (v39 to v44), all of it read from the game's own files, and all 24 bundled champion builds re-certified under the result.

**Now counted, for the first time:**

- **Critical hits.** Scrapper and Stalker criticals had never been scored. Counted at the chance the game itself states per power, taking the lower stated rate (the one against minions), never a guess.
- **Domination.** Control powers last 50 percent longer while it is up. Priced at the uptime the inherent's own numbers imply, reaching full uptime at the recharge players actually build to.
- **Self damage buffs.** Build Up, Aim, Rage, Follow Up, Soul Drain and more, priced at their real duration and recharge.
- **Absorb shields.** Sized and valued separately, and enhanced by the correct aspect.
- **Defence debuff resistance.** 178 powers. Super Reflexes had been taking a haircut it is specifically built to resist.
- **Slow resistance from powers.**
- **A dead lookup fixed:** recharge slotting was silently not reaching timed pets or click uptimes. Category Five, Auto Turret and Lightning Storm all price higher with recharge slotted, as they should.

**Corrections**, each checked against the game client: three Soul Drain records' target caps and radii, twenty-five control powers' durations and magnitudes, and thirteen pairs of mutually exclusive powers the tool now refuses to combine, just as the game does.

**Your builds:** all 24 bundled champions re-certified. Every one can be built in game exactly as shown, and each records the model version that certified it. The Spines/Fiery Aura farm build's score is lower than before: that is a more accurate price, not a worse build.

**Deliberately not counted, and said so:** mez protection (needs a decision about how often content mezzes you), the higher critical rate against bosses (needs an enemy rank mix the model does not track), and Domination's magnitude half (the game's files are ambiguous about it). Understatement over guesswork, as always.

**Retracted before release:** an earlier draft of this version claimed Wind Control, the Gadgetry and Utility Belt pools, and Boomerang Slice. None of them are live in the game. They were added from client files that carry unreleased content, and all of it was removed before anything shipped. A new standing check now compares the tool's data against the last shipped release, so content the game does not offer cannot slip in again unnoticed.

Game data is unchanged (Homecoming 2026.1.1242, current with the July 7 patch). The optimizer's model moves from v38 to v44, so build scores will differ from 0.12.35 in both directions, because the pricing is closer to the game.
